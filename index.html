About the Author

I’m Malhar Kharat — and I didn’t start writing because it looked cool. I started because I needed clarity. I needed something that felt real when motivation was gone.

My background is built on structured thinking, problem-solving, and discipline — and I bring that same energy into my books: practical ideas, clean thinking, and simple actions that compound over time.

A lot of my work comes from living through uncertainty: learning how to rebuild momentum, how to stop overthinking, and how to become someone you can trust — not just for one week, but for years.

When I’m not writing, I’m training, building projects, learning, and refining the habits that make progress feel normal.
