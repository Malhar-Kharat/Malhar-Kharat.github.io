About the Author

A personal story: why I write, what I studied, and what I'm building.

Malhar Kharat

Writer. Builder. Discipline-first.

I didn't start writing because it looked cool. I started writing because I needed clarity, something that still works when motivation disappears.

For a long time, I was the kind of person who wanted big results but kept fighting inconsistency: overthinking, starting late, stopping early, and restarting again. Writing became my way of turning chaos into systems and systems into progress.

My books are built for people who are tired of hype and want something real: simple actions, repeated honestly, until self-trust becomes your normal.

Studies & credibility

I have an engineering background, which heavily shapes how I write: structured thinking, problem-solving, and building systems that work in real life.

Master's : Automotive Engineering (Electric Vehicles focus) Background : Automotive Technology Engineering Style : Practical, systems-based writing

In engineering, you don't get results by "feeling motivated" — you get results by showing up, measuring, improving, and repeating. That same discipline is the foundation of my work.
