About the Author

A personal story: why I write, what I studied, and what I'm building.

Malhar Kharat

Writer. Builder. Discipline-first.

I spent my twenties trying to make big things happen and mostly failing at small, boring things: showing up, finishing what I started, not quitting in week three.

Then I got a master's in electric vehicle engineering, where none of that works. A drivetrain doesn't care how motivated you felt when you built it. It either survives the test cycle or it doesn't. That standard is what my books are built on: not motivation, repetition that holds up when nobody's checking.

Studies & credibility

My day job is engineering electric vehicle systems. That means I don't write about discipline as a feeling, I write about it as an input you can test, measure, and repeat, the same way you'd validate a drivetrain.

Master's : Automotive Engineering (Electric Vehicles focus) Background : Automotive Technology Engineering Style : Practical, systems-based writing

In engineering, you don't get results by "feeling motivated." You get results by showing up, measuring, improving, and repeating. That same discipline is the foundation of my work.
